# Visual design sources

These sources informed the bundled themes and layout rules. They are references,
not templates to copy verbatim.

## SciencePlots

https://github.com/garrettj403/SciencePlots

Adopted ideas:

- small, deliberate publication typography;
- thin axes and line weights;
- tight export padding;
- redundant color and line-style encodings;
- explicit journal-aware figure sizing.

## D2

https://github.com/d2lang/d2

Adopted ideas:

- a theme changes presentation without changing diagram semantics;
- nested containers use progressively quieter surfaces;
- palettes define neutral, primary, and accent roles rather than unrelated
  colors;
- technical edges need a dedicated neutral color.

## NN-SVG and PlotNeuralNet

https://github.com/alexlenail/NN-SVG  
https://github.com/HarisIqbal88/PlotNeuralNet

Adopted ideas:

- parameterized network drawings remain editable and reproducible;
- tensor depth, repeated layers, and dimensions need distinct visual grammar;
- SVG is a suitable publication source, not merely a preview format.

## PaperVizAgent

https://github.com/google-research/papervizagent

Adopted ideas:

- plan, style, render, and critique are separate phases;
- modern research diagrams favor soft scientific pastels and rounded process
  modules;
- line styles communicate forward, auxiliary, and feedback flows.

## Nature Research Figure Guide

https://research-figure-guide.nature.com/

Adopted ideas:

- figures must be designed at their final physical size;
- accessibility, integrity, export format, and resolution are part of figure
  quality;
- visual editing must not alter the underlying scientific record.
